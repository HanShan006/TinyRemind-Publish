## 📖 Overview / 简介

**TinyRemind** is a personal reminder and habit-building app for iOS. Instead of tapping through forms, you just **speak a sentence** — "Remind me to take vitamins tomorrow at 8 AM" — and AI figures out the title, time, repeat rule, and alert timing. When the moment comes, a **system-level alarm** rings on time, even in Silent mode.

> 小提醒是一款 iOS 端的提醒与习惯养成应用。你不必逐一填写表单，只需**说一句话**——"明天早上 8 点提醒我吃维生素"——AI 就会自动识别标题、时间、重复方式与提醒时机。到点时，**系统级闹钟**准时响铃，即使在静音模式下也不会错过。

## ✨ Core Features / 核心功能

- 🎙️ **AI Voice Creation / AI 语音创建** — Say it, no typing. AI extracts title, time, repeat rule and alert timing. Review line by line, then create with one tap. *(说一句即可，无需打字；AI 自动识别并支持逐行核对后一键创建)*
- ⏰ **System Alarms / 系统级闹钟** — Rings like a real alarm, dismissable from the Lock Screen, audible in Silent mode. *(锁屏可关闭，静音模式也能响；需 iOS 26.1+)*
- ✨ **Habit Tracking / 习惯打卡** — Custom icons, colors, categories. One-tap check-in, streak calendar, numeric check-ins and trend charts.
- 🎯 **Goal Management / 目标管理** — Link habits to goals and watch progress update automatically.
- 💊 **Medication / 用药提醒** — Scheduled reminders with taken / skipped / missed tracking and completion stats.
- 🎁 **Anniversaries / 纪念日提醒** — Advance reminders, countdowns and lunar calendar support.
- 📊 **Statistics / 数据统计** — Today's overview, weekly/monthly rates and trend charts.
- ☁️ **iCloud Sync / iCloud 同步** — Encrypted, automatic, multi-device. Works fully offline.

**Pro / Pro 版:** ad-free · unlimited habits & reminders · priority support.
*(无广告 · 无限习惯与提醒 · 优先支持)*

## ❓ FAQ / 常见问题

**Q: Does the alarm work on Silent mode? / 静音模式下还会响吗？**
A: Yes. On iOS 26.1+ reminders ring through the system alarm, which overrides Silent mode.

**Q: Which languages does AI voice support? / AI 语音支持哪些语言？**
A: English and Chinese.

**Q: Do I need an account? / 需要注册账号吗？**
A: No. TinyRemind works without sign-up; iCloud sync uses your own Apple ID.

**Q: Can I export my data? / 可以导出数据吗？**
A: Yes — export to CSV or JSON anytime from settings.

## 🔐 Privacy / 隐私

All reminder, habit, goal, medication and anniversary data is stored **locally on your device**, and — only if you enable iCloud sync — in **your private iCloud database**. We do not collect personal information and require no account. The app is fully functional offline.

> 所有数据均存储于**设备本地**，仅在开启 iCloud 同步时存于你**私有的 iCloud 数据库**。我们不收集个人信息，无需账号，可完全离线使用。
